# Step 55's stale-session model, after the two answers to the three answers' questions

Started from `plans/models/step-55/` on `main` (the three answers' round's, PR #3 `1c27277`). Both answers are in by default; there are no options for them any more. What changed:

- `model/machine.rs`, `Sim::class`: the second row takes a `22P02` from `enum_in` from any cached statement (break 120: only one that casts a parameter, as before), always one that casts no parameter to a non-built-in type, and always statement 10, which casts parameters to two types (break 122: the name test reads it).
- `model/rule.rs`, `is`: a name printed with no schema names only a cast written with none (break 121: any cast of that name, as before). `exhaustive` now counts a bare print of a cast written with its schema as the second row's; `from_file` takes two more kinds, `second` (the decision sends it to the second row) and `left` (the case the decision leaves).
- `model/core.rs`: statement 10 (`TWO`), whose stale cast's message, after a hand-made change, names the type it also casts to, as after two enums' names were exchanged or a composite or domain over the enum was kept beside its cast; statement 9 (`NOC`, no parameter cast) and 10 are in the load. A kept type of a cast written with its schema, while that schema is off the `search_path`, is moved by `SET SCHEMA` to a schema the path finds, printing with none, in a quarter of its swaps (`how` 3); `OPT=onpath` makes every type `app.st` and every kept swap so, and is also the setting "a cast written with its schema, its kept type moved where the search_path finds it".
- `model/calls.rs`: I14 lets the second row take, with `Error::SchemaChanged`, a bad label of a statement it always takes. The given-up case behind PgBouncer lets the parameter test's call through, as straight to Postgres. A new check, "R-1271 cost": a bad label, bad stored text or a kept function's error the second row takes never gets `Error::SchemaChanged` in a transaction with no retry from a run begun while its statement's pending entry exists (break 107, pending runs cached, fails it).
- `model/machine.rs`, after `on_error`: I9's loud half as decided. A kept type's stale function's error reached by a cached statement that casts no parameter gets the second row first, one fresh run outside a transaction and `SchemaChanged` and an entry inside one; it is never session class (switch 33 fails that); from a run that isn't cached it comes back as it is with nothing done.
- `OPT=nonoc`, `OPT=notwo` take statements 9 and 10 out of the load; `OPT=onpath` as above. `OPT=nocast`, `wide` and `strict` are gone.
- `model/breaks.py` adds 120 to 122 at their own settings (`set120` to `set122`), at the defaults and at the `onpath` setting, and 107 at "transactions without retry".
- `model/cost.sh`: runs per call and `Error::SchemaChanged` for bad text at the load, beside each answer undone.

`bash model/build.sh` builds to `.claude/scratch/wip/model` (the worktree's root); `model <setting|all|faults|setNN> <switch or break> <seed0> <count> [straight|pooled|both]`; `V=1` prints counts, `T=` threads. `python3 model/breaks.py <binary>`; `bash model/full.sh`.
